Our Transportation Services

Whatever your mobility needs, we have a vehicle and trained crew ready to get you there safely and on time.

Wheelchair Transport

Wheelchair-Accessible Rides

Our fleet includes ramp and lift-equipped vehicles designed for safe, comfortable wheelchair transport. Every wheelchair is properly secured for the entire trip.

  • Ramp and hydraulic lift-equipped vehicles
  • Secure wheelchair tie-down restraint systems
  • Door-to-door assistance from our trained drivers
  • Ideal for dialysis, physical therapy, and routine appointments
Request a Wheelchair Ride
Gurney & Stretcher Transport

Gurney & Stretcher Transport

For patients who must travel lying down, our gurney vans and trained crew provide safe, non-emergency stretcher transport between facilities, appointments, and home.

  • Non-emergency stretcher and gurney transport
  • Trained two-person crew for safe transfers
  • Facility-to-facility and facility-to-home transport
  • Careful handling for patients with limited mobility
Request a Gurney Ride
Ambulatory Transport

Ambulatory Transport

For patients who can walk but need a little extra help and supervision, we provide attentive door-through-door service for appointments and errands.

  • Door-through-door assistance
  • Support for patients with balance or mild mobility limitations
  • Friendly, patient, and punctual drivers
  • Great for outpatient visits, labs, and pharmacy stops
Request an Ambulatory Ride
Special Event Transport

Special Event Transportation

Weddings, birthdays, holidays, and family gatherings — we help your loved ones get to the moments that matter, safely and comfortably.

  • Wheelchair, gurney, and ambulatory options available
  • Flexible scheduling for one-time or recurring events
  • Round-trip and wait-and-return options
  • Extra care and patience for group outings
