Our On-Time Guarantee

A missed pickup can mean a missed appointment. We built our entire operation around not letting that happen.

The Numbers

99.1% On-Time Performance

Since 2013, American Medical Transportation has tracked every pickup we make. Today, we arrive within the scheduled pickup window 99.1% of the time — a standard we hold ourselves to on every single ride.

We know our passengers are often on their way to dialysis, chemotherapy, physical therapy, or other appointments where timing truly matters. That's why on-time performance isn't just a marketing number for us — it's how we schedule our drivers, plan our routes, and train our team.

Our Promise To You

What Our On-Time Guarantee Means

When you book a ride with American Medical Transportation, here's what you can expect from us.

We Show Up When Scheduled

Your ride is scheduled with a confirmed pickup window, and our dispatch team tracks every vehicle to keep it on time.

We Communicate Delays

If something outside our control causes a delay, we call ahead so you're never left wondering.

We Make It Right

If we fail to meet our on-time commitment on a scheduled ride, contact us and we'll work with you to make it right.

Guarantee terms apply to rides scheduled in advance with confirmed pickup windows. Call our team for full details.
