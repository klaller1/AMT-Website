About American Medical Transportation

Local, family-focused non-emergency medical transportation since 2013.

Our Story

Serving Our Community Since 2013

American Medical Transportation was founded to solve a simple but important problem: patients and families needed a transportation provider they could actually count on to show up, on time, every time.

For over a decade, we've provided wheelchair, gurney, and ambulatory transport for medical appointments, treatments, and special occasions across the San Gabriel Valley and San Bernardino County. As we grow, our commitment stays the same — treat every passenger like family, and never compromise on punctuality or safety.

  • 2013 American Medical Transportation is founded to serve local families with dependable non-emergency medical transportation.
  • Today Serving the San Gabriel Valley and San Bernardino County with a 99.1% on-time performance record.
  • What's Next Expanding our service area across greater Los Angeles County.
